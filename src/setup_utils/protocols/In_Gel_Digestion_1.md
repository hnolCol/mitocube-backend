# In Gel Digestion

## Summary
This protocol describes the digestion of proteins directly within a polyacrylamide gel piece, converting them into peptides suitable for LC-MS analysis. The gel piece is first washed and destained, proteins are reduced and alkylated in-gel, and then digested with Trypsin/LysC. Resulting peptides are extracted from the gel matrix, dried down, and prepared for desalting.

## Purpose
Used to process protein samples that have been separated or stored in polyacrylamide gels (e.g., after SDS-PAGE), enabling extraction and identification of proteins via mass spectrometry.

## Equipment
- Speed Vac (Concentrator Plus, Eppendorf)
- Centrifuge 5430 R (Eppendorf)
- Thermomixer C (Eppendorf, 96-well block)
- 1.5 mL tubes
- 96-well Deep Well Plates (500 µL)

## Buffers, Solutions
- 100% ACN
- 50 mM ABC (Ammonium bicarbonate)
- 50% 50 mM ABC / 50% ACN
- 30% ACN / 3% TFA
- Acetonitrile (VWR, 83642.290)
- Ammonium bicarbonate (Sigma, 09830-500G)
- Trifluoroacetic acid (Sigma, T6508-500mL)
- TCEP, 0.5 M stock — Tris-(2-carboxyethyl)-phosphine hydrochloride solution (Sigma, 646547-10x1mL)
- CAA, 1 M stock — 2-Chloroacetamide (Sigma, C0267-500g)
- Lys C, 1 µg/µL (Lysyl Endopeptidase, Fujifilm Wako Chemicals Europe, 129-02541; resuspended in LC/MS water)
- Trypsin, 1 µg/µL (Sigma, T6567-1mg; resuspended in 1 mM HCl)

## Procedure

### 1. Initial Washing
1. Add 100 µL 50% 50 mM ABC / 50% ACN to samples (prepare buffer fresh)
2. Incubate 20 min, RT
3. Discard solution
   - *Tip:* When discarding solution, use a 1000 µL or 200 µL pipette with a 10 µL tip attached on top, so no gel piece is sucked into the tip
4. Add 100 µL 50% 50 mM ABC / 50% ACN to samples
5. Incubate 20 min, RT
6. Discard solution

### 2. Dehydration
1. Add 150 µL 100% acetonitrile
2. Vortex briefly
3. Incubate 15 min
4. Discard solution
5. Dry in SpeedVac for 10 min

### 3. Reduction
1. Cover gel pieces with 100 µL TCEP (10 mM final concentration)
2. Check that gel pieces are fully covered and no air bubbles are visible
3. Incubate 30 min, RT

### 4. Alkylation
1. Add CAA (55 mM final concentration)
2. Incubate 30 min, RT, in the dark
3. Discard solution

### 5. Washing/Destaining
1. Cover gel pieces with 100 µL ABC
2. Incubate 15 min
3. Discard solution
4. Cover gel pieces with 150 µL ACN
5. Vortex briefly
6. Incubate 10 min
7. Discard solution
8. Dry gel pieces in SpeedVac (approx. 10 min)

### 6. Complete Destaining (if needed)
*Not necessary if pieces are already destained.*
1. Add 100 µL 50 mM ABC
2. Incubate 15 min
3. Discard solution
4. Cover gel pieces with ACN
5. Incubate 15 min
6. Repeat steps until pieces are destained
7. Dry gel pieces in SpeedVac for 5 min

### 7. Digest
1. Prepare digestion solution: 10 ng/µL total (90% Trypsin, 10% LysC in 50 mM ABC)
2. Add digestion solution to cover the pieces (approx. 30 µL)
   - Let gel pieces swell for 30 min at 4 °C
3. Add 50 mM ABC solution to fully cover gel pieces (if necessary)
4. Incubate overnight at 37 °C, shaking
5. Transfer supernatant into a tube or directly into a new plate

### 8. Extraction of Peptide Digestion Products
1. Add 100 µL 30% ACN / 3% TFA, vortex
2. Incubate 20 min, RT
3. Transfer supernatant into same plate/tube as before (combine supernatants)
4. Add 150 µL 100% ACN, vortex briefly
5. Transfer supernatant into same plate/tube as before (combine supernatants)
6. Dry down collected supernatant in SpeedVac to remove organic solvent
   - Remaining volume is approx. <50 µL

### 9. Next Step
- Continue with StageTip protocol for desalting peptides
