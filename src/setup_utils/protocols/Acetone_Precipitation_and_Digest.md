# Acetone Precipitation and Digest

**Purpose:** Used to concentrate protein samples and/or remove interfering detergents prior to downstream processing (e.g., mass spectrometry sample prep).

## Equipment
- Thermomixer C (Eppendorf)
- Centrifuge 5430 R (Eppendorf)
- 1.5 mL tubes with safe lock
- LoProtein binding tubes

## Materials/Solutions
- 100% Acetone (ACN)
- 80% ACN
- 100 mM HEPES, pH 8.5
- HEPES (Sigma, H4034-100g)
- Lys C, 1 µg/µL (Lysyl Endopeptidase, Fujifilm Wako Chemicals Europe, 129-02541; resuspended in LC/MS water)
- Trypsin, 1 µg/µL (Sigma, T6567-1mg; resuspended in 1 mM HCl)
- TCEP, 0.5 M stock — Tris-(2-carboxyethyl)-phosphine hydrochloride solution (Sigma, 646547-10x1mL)
- CAA, 1 M stock — 2-Chloroacetamide (Sigma, C0267-500g)
- Trifluoroacetic acid (Sigma, T6508-500mL)

## Procedure

### 1. Reduction and Alkylation (before Acetone precipitation)
- **Reduction:** Add TCEP to 10 mM final concentration
  - Incubate 45 °C, 15 min, 550 rpm
- **Alkylation:** Add CAA to 20 mM final concentration
  - Keep CAA in the dark (light-sensitive)
  - Incubate 45 °C, 30 min, 550 rpm, in the dark

### 2. Acetone Precipitation
1. Add 4–5x volume of ice-cold 100% acetone
2. Incubate 1–2 h at –80 °C, or overnight at –20 °C
3. Centrifuge: 18,213 × g, 10 min, 4 °C
4. Discard supernatant (decant)

### 3. Washing
1. Wash with 500 µL ice-cold 80% acetone
2. Centrifuge: 18,213 × g, 5 min, 4 °C
3. Discard supernatant (decant)
4. Wash again with 500 µL ice-cold 80% acetone
5. Centrifuge: 18,213 × g, 5 min, 4 °C
6. Discard supernatant (decant and remove residual liquid with a pipet)
7. Let pellet air-dry under the fume hood (**max. 3 min** — longer drying makes the pellet difficult to resuspend)

### 4. Resuspension
- Add 50–100 µL of 100 mM HEPES, pH 8.5, to the pellet

### 5. Digest
1. Add Lys C (100:1 protein:enzyme ratio)
   - Incubate 2 h at 37 °C, 900 rpm
2. Add Trypsin (100:1 protein:enzyme ratio)
   - Resuspend pellet if necessary
   - Incubate overnight at 37 °C, 550 rpm

### 6. Stop Reaction / Clean-up
1. Add 10% TFA to a final concentration of 0.5–1%
2. Centrifuge at full speed: 18,213 × g, 10 min, RT
3. Transfer sample to a new tube or directly onto StageTips
4. Continue with desalting protocol (C18)
